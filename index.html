Progression guidelines

Increase resistance only when you can perform all prescribed sets at the top of the repetition range with good form and about 2–3 repetitions in reserve. For large jumps in available weights, progress through additional repetitions, slower lowering, or more challenging variations instead.

Avoid weighted jumps, sprints, and treadmill running with the vest. Single-dumbbell configurations above 5.5 lb are not listed until the equipment's safe capacity is confirmed.
